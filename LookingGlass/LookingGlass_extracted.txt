                                                                                                                                 prompts “an oil painting of a village” and “an oil painting
                                                                                                                                 of a horse” both share the style descriptor “an oil painting
                                                                                                                                 of”, which should not be included in the negative prompt.

                                                                                                                                 such as fur. If these shared features appear in both the nega-

                                                                                                                                 (from the other view). Thus, we subtract the positive prompt
                                                                                                                                 embedding from the negative one. Our experiments showed
